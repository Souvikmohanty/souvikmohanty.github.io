Personal Profile

About Me

I am Souvik, a passionate Entomologist dedicated to studying insects, their intricate behaviour, and their fundamental role in stabilizing and enhancing modern agricultural ecosystems.

Currently, I am pursuing my postgraduate studies as an M.Sc. Scholar in the Department of Entomology, Central Agricultural University (CAU), College of Agriculture, Imphal, Manipur. My work stands at the intersection of conservation biology and crop enhancement. I study native stingless bees from the remote, species-rich wilds of Northeast India—specifically centering my academic focus on the first biological documentation of the stingless bee species Tetragonula srikantanathi.

Beyond entomological observations, I research stingless bee nesting architectures and their pollination efficacy on commercially vital crops like rapeseed and mustard. My goal is to develop low-cost, sustainable domestication mechanisms (such as custom bamboo hives) that help native farmers maximize crop yields through biological pollination.

When I am not observing colonies in the laboratory or fields, I am an avid reader and writer, exploring the broader impacts of biological sciences, agriculture, and sustainable development.

Focus Species Spotlight

Tetragonula srikantanathi & Honey Benefits

Taxonomic Profile

The Wild Stingless Bee

Species: Tetragonula srikantanathi

Native to the dense forest reserves and remote valleys of Northeast India, T. srikantanathi is a key generalist pollinator. My research represents the first comprehensive biological documentation of this species, examining its developmental stages, foraging behaviors, and nesting structures.

Medicinal Honey Benefits ("Putka" / Dammar Honey)

  • Rich Bioactive Compounds: Packed with twice the concentration of phenolic compounds, organic acids, and flavonoids compared to standard honeybee (Apis) honey, yielding superior antioxidant potential.
  • Strong Antibacterial & Healing Action: High concentrations of naturally occurring hydrogen peroxide and organic acids make it a potent topical treatment for skin lesions, sore throats, and eye therapies.
  • Trehalulose Sugar (Low-Glycemic): Naturally rich in trehalulose—a rare sugar characterized by slow absorption, providing a lower glycemic load and protecting oral health.
  • Tangy Acid-Balanced Taste: Features a unique sweet-sour flavor profile due to natural fermentation by crop-associated lactic acid bacteria.

Insect Monitoring & Control Device

Field of Invention: Agricultural Engineering / Entomology

An innovative, field-deployable electro-mechanical apparatus designed for continuous surveillance and eco-friendly population management of key crop pests. The system uses specific behavioral triggers to monitor insect frequency, allowing farmers to apply targeted treatments, reduce reliance on chemical sprays, and prevent pest resistance.

  • Eco-Friendly Control: Integrated suppression mechanics minimize accidental capture of beneficial pollinators.
